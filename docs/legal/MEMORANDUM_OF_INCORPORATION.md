## Part C — Shares and Capital

6. **Authorised shares:** The Company is authorised to issue **1,000 (one thousand) ordinary shares**, all of the same class, with no par value, carrying equal voting rights and equal rights to dividends and distributions.

7. **Issued shares:** At incorporation, **1 (one) ordinary share** is issued to the Founder, MK Ditshego, for a consideration of R1.00 (one rand).

8. **Future issues:** The Board may issue further shares up to the authorised limit by ordinary resolution of the Board, provided that:
   - Any issue to a person who is not an existing shareholder must first be offered to existing shareholders in proportion to their holdings (pre-emptive right);
   - The pre-emptive right lapses if not exercised within 30 business days of written notice;
   - The pre-emptive right may be waived by written consent of all existing shareholders.

9. **Transfer of shares:** A shareholder wishing to transfer shares must first offer them to existing shareholders (right of first refusal) at a fair market value determined by agreement or, failing agreement, by an independent expert. Transfers to non-shareholders require written Board approval.

10. **Drag-along right:** If shareholders holding more than 75% of issued shares agree to sell the Company to a bona fide third-party acquirer, they may require remaining shareholders to sell their shares on the same terms.

11. **Tag-along right:** If any shareholder proposes to sell shares to a third party, remaining shareholders have the right to participate in the sale on the same terms, pro rata to their holdings.

## Part D — Directors

12. **Minimum directors:** The Company must have at least 1 (one) director at all times.

13. **First director:** MK Ditshego is the first director of the Company.

14. **Appointment:** Directors are appointed by ordinary resolution of shareholders. The Founder may appoint and remove directors without shareholder approval while holding more than 50% of issued shares.

15. **Powers of the Board:** The Board has full authority to manage the business of the Company, including the power to:
    - Borrow money and pledge Company assets as security;
    - Enter into contracts on behalf of the Company;
    - Appoint and remove officers, employees, and agents;
    - Declare dividends;
    - Approve the Company's annual budget and financial statements.

16. **Directors' interests:** A director who has a material personal interest in a matter before the Board must disclose that interest, recuse themselves from deliberation, and not vote on the matter.

17. **Directors' remuneration:** Directors may be remunerated for their services to the Company as determined by the Board, provided that executive remuneration is approved annually.

29. **Protection:** The Company must take reasonable steps to protect its intellectual property, including registration of trademarks, patents, and copyrights where appropriate.
