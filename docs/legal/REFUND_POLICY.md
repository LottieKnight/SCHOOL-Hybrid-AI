# SCHOOL Platform — Refund and Credits Policy

**Effective Date:** 26 May 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

> **Summary:** Wallet credits are non-refundable once used. Unused credits are refundable on account closure if the balance exceeds R10. Tutoring session fees are partially refundable depending on when cancellation occurs. The Consumer Protection Act gives you additional rights that this policy cannot override.

---

## 1. Consumer Protection Act Rights

This policy operates alongside and does not override your rights under the **Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 ("CPA")**. Where any provision of this policy conflicts with the CPA, the CPA prevails.

Your CPA rights include but are not limited to:
- The right to cancel a service booking within 5 business days without penalty if SCHOOL did not comply with Section 43 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act;
- The right to fair and honest disclosure about all charges before you purchase;
- The right to goods and services of acceptable quality;
- The right to approach the **National Consumer Tribunal** or a **consumer court** in your province if your rights are violated.

## 2. Wallet Credits

### 2.1 Nature of Credits

Wallet credits are a prepaid digital currency used within the SCHOOL Platform. They are not:
- Legal tender;
- A bank product or financial service product regulated by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority;
- Transferable between user accounts.

### 2.2 Unused Credits — Refund on Account Closure

If you close your account and hold an unused credit balance equivalent to **R10.00 or more**, you may request a cash refund of that balance before account closure.

**To request a refund of unused credits:**
1. Navigate to Account Settings → Wallet → Request Refund before closing your account.
2. Alternatively, email billing@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za with your account email and the refund request.
3. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within **7–14 business days**.
4. SCHOOL may deduct any outstanding amounts owed to SCHOOL from the refund.

Unused credit balances below R10.00 are not refundable due to processing cost constraints.

### 2.3 Credits Used for Services

Credits that have been used to unlock past paper reports, AI sessions, or other Platform features are **not refundable** unless:
- The feature failed to function as described and you reported the failure within 7 days; or
- A refund is required under the Consumer Protection Act.

### 2.4 Expired Credits

Credits expire only on account closure. Credits do not expire while your account is active.

## 3. Tutoring Sessions

### 3.1 Cancellation by Learner

| Cancellation Timing | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before the cancellation window (more than **6 hours** before session) | Full refund to wallet credits |
| Within the cancellation window (within 6 hours of session) | 70% refund; 30% cancellation fee retained |
| No-show (learner does not join within 15-minute grace period) | 50% of session fee paid to tutor; 50% returned to learner wallet |

### 3.2 Cancellation by Tutor

If a tutor cancels a session for any reason:
- **Full refund** to the learner's wallet credits within 24 hours.
- The tutor receives no payment for the cancelled session.

### 3.3 Tutor No-Show

If a tutor fails to attend a session and the learner waits beyond the 15-minute grace period:
- **Full refund** to the learner's wallet credits within 24 hours.
- A strike is recorded on the tutor's account.

### 3.4 Disputed Sessions

If you raise a dispute within the 48-hour dispute window after a session, SCHOOL will review the dispute and may issue a partial or full credit refund at its discretion. Our dispute decision is final within the Platform but does not affect your rights under the CPA.

## 4. Bookstore and Digital Products

### 4.1 Digital Content

Digital content (e.g., unlocked past paper detailed reports) is delivered immediately upon purchase. Under Section 16(3)(b) of the CPA, you acknowledge that by requesting immediate delivery of digital content, you waive the cooling-off period for that specific transaction.

If digital content was not delivered or was materially defective, contact support@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za within 7 days for a credit adjustment.

### 4.2 Physical Products

Equipment procurement is currently quote-only. No payment for physical goods is processed through the Platform at this time.

## 5. Subscription and Recurring Charges

If SCHOOL introduces subscription tiers in future, the following terms will apply:
- You may cancel a subscription at any time through account settings;
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle;
- No pro-rated refunds are issued for the remaining period of a cancelled subscription, unless required by the CPA;
- If SCHOOL cancels your subscription for any reason other than breach of Terms, a pro-rated refund will be issued for the unused portion.

---

## 6. Payment Processing Errors

If your payment was charged but credits were not added to your wallet, contact billing@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za within 7 days. We will investigate and credit your account or issue a refund as appropriate.

---

## 7. How to Request a Refund

1. **Email:** billing@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za
2. Include your account email, the transaction reference, and the reason for your request.
3. We will acknowledge your request within **2 business days** and resolve it within **7 business days**.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the **National Consumer Tribunal** (www.thenct.org.za) or the **Consumer Goods and Services Ombud** (www.cgso.org.za).
