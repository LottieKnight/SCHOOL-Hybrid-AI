## The Vision

DITSHEGO GROUP is not a company. It is a legacy.

It is structured on the Ludo principle: **all pieces move together, protect each other, and cross the finish line as a unit.** No subsidiary is left behind. No division operates in isolation. Every rand earned inside the Group is reinvested inside the Group first.

The structure is modelled after the most durable South African conglomerates — Naspers, Remgro, PSG — with a modern, multi-sector design that compounds advantage across technology, media, commerce, finance, entertainment, philanthropy, real estate, and trade.

## Layer 1 — THE CROWN: DITSHEGO GROUP (Pty) Ltd

**Role:** Apex holding company. Owns 100% of all subsidiaries at formation.
Does not trade directly. Receives dividends from subsidiaries. Provides group-level governance, capital allocation, and strategic direction.

**Why this is the crown:**
- All inter-company dividends flow here **tax-free** (Section 64F, Income Tax Act)
- Capital raised here can be deployed to any subsidiary as equity or a loan
- If any subsidiary fails, the Group's other assets are protected from its creditors
- Selling the entire Group in future = one transaction at Group level
- The MK Ditshego name and legacy is attached to this entity, not to any single business

**Structure at formation:**
- 1,000 authorised ordinary shares
- 1 share issued to MK Ditshego
- Financial year end: 28 February
- MK Ditshego: sole director and shareholder

**Name to register:** `Ditshego Group` (Pty) Ltd ← Register this FIRST on BizPortal

### Cryptic SPHERE (Pty) Ltd

**Role:** Algorithmic trading and financial technology. Operates the Orchestrator trading engine. Engages in proprietary trading using AI-powered ICT sweep models.

**Critical legal note — FSCA licensing:**
If Cryptic SPHERE manages or invests **third-party money**, it requires a **Financial Services Provider (FSP) licence** from the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) under the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act 37 of 2002 (FAIS).
- **Proprietary trading only (own capital):** No FSP licence required
- **Managing client funds or giving investment advice:** FSP licence required (Category I or II)
- **Recommendation:** Start as proprietary trading only. Obtain FSP licence before accepting investor capital.

**Cross-Group synergies:**
- Trading algorithms owned by AURA AI Labs (pays licence fee)
- Investment capital seeded by Ditshego Group
- Profits return to Ditshego Group as dividends (tax-free)

## Layer 5 — FUTURE CLUSTER

### Ditshego Properties (Pty) Ltd

**Role:** Real estate investment company. The Monopoly strategy applied with discipline.

**The Strategy (exactly as you described):**

```
Phase 1: Buy small residential properties
         ↓
Phase 2: Rent them out (income + appreciation)
         ↓
Phase 3: Refinance (use equity to buy more, DON'T sell — no CGT)
         ↓
Phase 4: Portfolio grows → bank lending improves → terms improve
         ↓
Phase 5: Sell small portfolio → buy one medium commercial property
         ↓
Phase 6: Repeat: commercial → bigger commercial
```

**Why never sell unless forced:**
- CGT on company property: 80% inclusion rate × 27% = **21.6% effective CGT**
- Refinancing is tax-free — you access equity without a taxable event
- Hold the asset, keep the equity, keep the rental income flowing

**Key tax advantages:**
- **Section 13sex:** 5% per year depreciation allowance on cost of **new** residential properties let out (building structure only)
- **Section 11(a):** Rates, levies, insurance, maintenance, bond interest are all deductible
- **Section 11(e):** Wear and tear on fittings
- **VAT on commercial property:** If VAT registered, claim input VAT on purchase price of commercial property

**Starting point — Vanderbijlpark, Gauteng:**
- Target: affordable townhouses/flats near Vaal University of Technology (VUT) or NWU Vaal campus
- Student accommodation is high-demand, low-vacancy
- Rental income supports SCHOOL platform users (the same demographic)
- Purchase price target: R400k–R800k for first property (R30k–R60k deposit at 10%)

**Funding strategy:**
- Ditshego Group provides seed capital as a shareholder loan to Ditshego Properties
- Property is bonded (mortgage); rental income services the bond
- Group's Cryptic SPHERE trading profits can fund deposits

## Layer 6 — PHILANTHROPY

### GETTING STARTED NPC (Non-Profit Company)

**Role:** The Group's philanthropic arm. Education, youth development, entrepreneurship enablement.

**Legal structure:**
- **NPC** = Non-Profit Company registered under the Companies Act 71 of 2008 (NOT a Section 21 company — that was the old Act)
- NPC has no shareholders; has members and directors
- Governed by a Memorandum of Incorporation with an altruistic object clause
- Registration at CIPC: CoR14.1 — costs R175 same as Pty Ltd
- ADDITIONALLY register as an **NPO** with the Department of Social Development (free; gives credibility)
- ADDITIONALLY apply for **Section 18A** approval from SARS — this allows donors to claim tax deductions on donations to GETTING STARTED

**The Section 18A Advantage:**
```
Ditshego Group donates R100,000 to GETTING STARTED NPC
→ Group gets a R100,000 tax deduction (saves R27,000 in company tax)
→ GETTING STARTED gets R100,000 to do good
→ Net cost to Group: R73,000 to do R100,000 of social good
```

This is not avoidance — it is the designed incentive in the Income Tax Act.

**Objects (draft):**
- Provide educational resources and bursaries to students who cannot afford them
- Enable young entrepreneurs from underserved communities to start businesses
- Provide technology access (devices, internet) to disadvantaged learners
- Support the SCHOOL platform's lite-mode zero-rated access for data-constrained students

**Governance requirement:**
- NPC must have at least 3 directors (incorporators)
- NPC cannot pay dividends; all surplus reinvested in the objects
- Annual financial statements filed with CIPC
- NPO reports filed with Department of Social Development annually

## The Ludo Strategy — Cross-Investment Framework

Every Group company trades with every other Group company under formal written agreements. This is legal, tax-efficient, and binding.

```
INTERNAL SERVICE AGREEMENT MAP:

AURA AI Labs ─────→ licences AI tech to → SCHOOL, Cryptic SPHERE, AURA STUDIOS, Media House
SCHOOL ───────────→ hosts marketplace for → Cosmic Cotton, Flightmode
SCHOOL ───────────→ zero-rated access for → GETTING STARTED beneficiaries
Cryptic SPHERE ───→ returns profits to → Ditshego Group (dividends, tax-free)
Ditshego Group ───→ donates to → GETTING STARTED NPC (Section 18A deduction)
Media House ──────→ markets for → ALL Group companies (internal rate)
Record Label ─────→ provides music to → AURA STUDIOS (sync licence), V4NTAG3 (live), Media House (radio)
V4NTAG3 ──────────→ uses → AURA STUDIOS games, clothing brand merch, Record Label artists
Ditshego Trade ───→ sources for → Cosmic Cotton, Flightmode, SCHOOL equipment, V4NTAG3 events
Ditshego Properties→ provides → Group HQ office space (lease to Ditshego Group)
DITSHEGO SPORTS ──→ covered by → Media House, kits from clothing brands, events by V4NTAG3
```

**Why this matters legally and financially:**
1. Each internal transaction is a real contract at arm's length pricing
2. The paying entity gets a tax deduction; the receiving entity declares the income
3. Money stays inside the Group — it circulates before it ever leaves
4. SARS can audit internal pricing; keep all agreements at market rates to avoid transfer pricing disputes

## The Legacy Layer

DITSHEGO GROUP is the name that outlives any single product. Products change. Technologies become obsolete. Markets shift. But a Group with a strong enough holding structure, diversified enough assets, and a clear enough mission can outlast any one venture.

The Ludo strategy means: **when one piece is ahead, it slows down and waits.** When one company has a strong year, it invests in the ones that are still building. The Group wins when the Group wins — not when one piece wins alone.
