# SCHOOL Platform — Copyright and Content Policy

**Effective Date:** 26 May 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Overview

This policy sets out how SCHOOL handles intellectual property, including:
- Curriculum and past paper content derived from the Department of Basic Education ("DBE");
- User-submitted content;
- Third-party content licensed to the Platform;
- Copyright infringement reports and the takedown procedure.

---

## 2. South African Copyright Framework

The primary copyright law governing content on the Platform is the **Copyright Act 98 of 1978** (as amended). SCHOOL operates in compliance with this Act and its applicable exemptions.

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("**DMCA**") of the United States also applies to our infrastructure provider (Railway Corporation, based in the United States). SCHOOL maintains a DMCA takedown procedure (Section 6 below) in addition to the SA copyright procedure.

---

## 3. Department of Basic Education (DBE) and CAPS Content

### 3.1 Government Copyright

Past papers, memoranda, and CAPS curriculum documents published by the **Department of Basic Education** are works of the South African Government and are subject to copyright under the Copyright Act, with the Government of South Africa as the copyright owner.

### 3.2 SCHOOL's Use of DBE Materials

SCHOOL ingests and makes available DBE past papers for the following educational purposes, which we consider to fall within the educational use provisions of the Copyright Act and to be in the public interest given the DBE's stated commitment to open access to educational materials:

- Enabling learners to practise against real past examinations;
- Providing AI-assisted marking and feedback on learner attempts;
- Identifying gaps in learner knowledge aligned to the CAPS curriculum.

SCHOOL does not claim ownership of DBE content and clearly attributes the source on all past paper materials.

### 3.3 DBE Takedown

If the Department of Basic Education or any authorised agent requests the removal of specific content, SCHOOL will comply promptly. Contact: legal@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za.

## 4. SCHOOL Original Content

All original content created by SCHOOL — including lesson explanations, quiz questions not sourced from DBE, AI-generated summaries, and Platform interface elements — is the intellectual property of Ditshego Ventures (Pty) Ltd t/a SCHOOL and is protected by the Copyright Act.

Users may not reproduce, distribute, or commercially exploit SCHOOL original content without express written permission.

## 5. User-Submitted Content

Users may submit notes, answers, community posts, and other content ("User Content"). By submitting User Content, you warrant that:
- You own the content or have the right to submit it;
- The content does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights;
- The content does not include DBE past paper solutions obtained through unauthorised means.

The licence you grant SCHOOL over User Content is described in the Terms of Service, Section 6.1.

## 6. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content on the Platform infringes your copyright, you may submit a takedown notice under either the South African Copyright Act procedure or the DMCA procedure.

### 6.1 South African Copyright Act — Takedown Notice

Send your notice to:

**SCHOOL Copyright Agent**
Email: privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za
Physical: [Redacted for public repository — contact privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za]

Your notice must include:
1. Your full name, address, telephone number, and email address;
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed (or a description if a specific work cannot be identified);
3. Identification of the material on the Platform that you claim is infringing, including the URL or sufficient description to allow us to locate it;
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or law;
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate;
6. Your signature (electronic signature acceptable).

### 6.2 DMCA Takedown Notice (United States)

For DMCA purposes, SCHOOL's Designated Agent is:

**SCHOOL Copyright Agent**
Email: privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za
Address: [Redacted for public repository — contact privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za]

DMCA notices must comply with 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) and include the elements listed in Section 6.1 above, plus a declaration under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and that you are authorised to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

### 6.3 Processing of Takedown Notices

Upon receiving a valid notice:
1. We will acknowledge receipt within **2 business days**;
2. We will remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material within **5 business days** of verifying the notice is complete;
3. We will notify the uploader of the takedown.

Repeat infringers will have their accounts terminated.

## 7. Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice to privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za including:
1. Your name, address, and contact details;
2. Identification of the removed content and where it appeared before removal;
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. Your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of South Africa (for SA notices) or the applicable US federal court (for DMCA counter-notices).

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material within **10–14 business days** unless the complainant has filed a court action.

---

## 8. Fair Use and Educational Exemptions

The Copyright Act permits certain uses of copyrighted works for **educational purposes** without the need for a licence. SCHOOL relies on these exemptions for:
- Reproducing portions of published works for commentary, criticism, or classroom illustration;
- Enabling learners to practise with past examination papers for study purposes.

These exemptions are applied narrowly and in good faith.
