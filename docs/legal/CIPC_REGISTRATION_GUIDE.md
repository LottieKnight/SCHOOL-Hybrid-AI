## Step 4: Tax Structure — Choosing the Right Regime

Once registered, you need to elect your tax treatment. Here are your options:

### Option A: Normal Company Tax (Default)
- Rate: **27%** on taxable income
- Best if: revenue exceeds R20m/year

### Option B: Small Business Corporation (SBC) — **Recommended for Year 1**
- Qualifying criteria:
  - All shareholders must be natural persons (just you — qualifies)
  - Gross income < R20 million
  - Not a personal service company
  - No more than 20% of income from investments
- Tax rates (2026):
  - **0%** on first R95,750
  - **7%** on R95,751 – R365,000
  - **21%** on R365,001 – R550,000
  - **27%** above R550,000
- Additional benefit: **100% depreciation** on manufacturing assets in year of purchase (Section 12E)

### Option C: Turnover Tax (Micro Business) — Only if turnover < R1m
- Flat rate on turnover (not profit)
- Simplest but may pay tax even if making a loss
- Not recommended if you plan to scale quickly

**Verdict:** Register under the normal company tax regime and elect SBC status when you file your first tax return. SCHOOL's tech infrastructure costs (Railway, OpenAI, Stripe) are all deductible business expenses.
