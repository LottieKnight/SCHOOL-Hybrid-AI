# SCHOOL Platform — Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 May 2026
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit and preferences.

---

## 2. What Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the Platform to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `school_session` | Maintains your authenticated login session | 7 days |
| `school_csrf` | Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks | 7 days |
| `lite_mode` | Remembers your bandwidth/data-saving preference | 1 year |

### 2.2 Preference Cookies (Optional)

These cookies remember your settings and preferences.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Stores your display theme preference | 1 year |

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional — Consent Required)

These cookies help us understand how the Platform is used. We activate these only with your consent.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| None currently | No analytics provider in use | Not applicable — platform does not currently use analytics cookies | N/A |

### 2.4 Third-Party Cookies

Our payment processor (Stripe) may set cookies during the checkout flow. These are governed by Stripe's Cookie Policy at https://stripe.com/za/cookie-settings.

---

## 3. Your Cookie Choices

### 3.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit the Platform, you will be presented with a consent banner allowing you to accept or decline optional cookies. Essential cookies are always active.

### 3.2 Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw cookie consent at any time:
- Through the **Cookie Preferences** link in the Platform footer; or
- By clearing cookies in your browser settings (note: this will log you out of your session).

### 3.3 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Visit your browser's help documentation for instructions.

---

## 4. Zero-Rated Endpoints

Certain Platform endpoints are designated as **zero-rated** under ICASA data zero-rating guidelines for educational content:

- `/api/lesson-json`
- `/api/lessons/quiz/bias`
- `/api/lessons/quiz/attempt`

These endpoints operate without cookies where technically feasible to minimise data usage on mobile networks.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove cookies. Material changes will be notified through the Platform's cookie consent mechanism.
