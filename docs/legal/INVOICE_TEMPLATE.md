## Invoice Numbering System

Use the format: `INV-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN`

- `YYYY` = Year (e.g., 2026)
- `MM` = Month (e.g., 04)
- `DD` = Day (e.g., 30)
- `NNN` = Sequential number for that day, starting at 001

Example: `INV-2026-04-30-001` (your first invoice, which you already used)

**Why this matters:** Sequential invoice numbers are a SARS requirement for VAT vendors and are best practice for all businesses. They make tax audits clean.

---

## When to Use VAT

You currently do NOT need to charge VAT:
- Until your annual taxable turnover exceeds **R1,000,000**
- Use **Option A** (not VAT registered) on all invoices until you register

Once turnover approaches R1m, register voluntarily before the threshold (gives you input tax claims on your business expenses — actually beneficial).
