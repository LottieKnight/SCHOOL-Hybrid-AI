## 2. Independent Contractor Status

**The Tutor is an independent service provider and is not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venture of SCHOOL.**

This Agreement does not create any employment relationship between the Tutor and SCHOOL. Specifically:

- SCHOOL does not control how the Tutor prepares for, structures, or delivers tutoring sessions;
- SCHOOL does not set the Tutor's working hours beyond session scheduling agreed between Tutor and Learner;
- SCHOOL does not provide the Tutor with employment benefits of any kind, including medical aid, pension, UIF, or leave pay;
- **SCHOOL does not withhold Pay As You Earn (PAYE) tax** on amounts paid to the Tutor;
- The Tutor is solely responsible for all tax obligations arising from earnings on the Platform, including income tax, provisional tax, and VAT (where the Tutor is a registered VAT vendor).

SCHOOL operates as a **technology marketplace and facilitator**. The Tutor's service contract is with the Learner; SCHOOL facilitates payment and provides the Platform infrastructure.

## 3. Tutor Registration and Verification

### 3.1 Eligibility

To register as a Tutor, you must:
- Be at least 18 years of age;
- Be a natural person (companies and institutions may not register as individual tutors);
- Hold genuine qualifications relevant to the subjects you offer;
- Have a valid South African bank account capable of receiving electronic payments;
- Not be subject to any court order, professional sanction, or legal prohibition that would prevent you from working with learners.

### 3.2 Verification Documents

SCHOOL may require you to submit:
- A certified copy of your South African identity document or passport;
- Proof of academic qualifications;
- A Police Clearance Certificate (for tutors working regularly with minor learners);
- Proof of bank account.

You warrant that all documents and information provided are genuine and accurate. Submitting false documents is grounds for immediate termination and may be reported to relevant authorities.

### 3.3 Verification Status

Verification is displayed on your tutor profile. SCHOOL does not guarantee the accuracy of verification displays and recommends that Learners conduct their own due diligence.

## 4. Session Conduct

### 4.1 Tutor Obligations

You agree to:
- Be available and punctual for all accepted sessions;
- Deliver tutoring services with reasonable care and skill;
- Treat all Learners with respect, particularly minor Learners;
- Not share Learner personal information with third parties;
- Not attempt to conduct tutor-learner transactions outside the Platform to circumvent SCHOOL's commission;
- Comply with the **Acceptable Use Policy** in all communications with Learners.

### 4.2 Working with Minor Learners

When tutoring learners who are under 18 years of age, you must:
- Conduct sessions through the Platform's video/messaging tools only;
- Not request direct contact information from minor Learners;
- Not meet minor Learners in person outside of formal educational settings without parental consent;
- Report any safeguarding concerns to SCHOOL at safeguarding@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za and to relevant authorities as required by the **Children's Act 38 of 2005**.

## 5. Payment and Commission

### 5.1 Session Fees

Tutors set their own hourly rates on the Platform (subject to any minimum/maximum limits set by SCHOOL from time to time).

### 5.2 SCHOOL Commission

SCHOOL retains a commission of **12%** of each session fee. This commission is deducted before payout.

### 5.3 Payout Processing

Earnings are held by SCHOOL until the 48-hour dispute window following a completed session has closed. Payouts are processed within **7 business days** to your registered bank account.

### 5.4 Cancellation and No-Show

| Scenario | Tutor Payment |
|---|---|
| Learner cancels within cancellation window | 30% of session fee (cancellation fee) |
| Learner no-show | 50% of session fee |
| Tutor cancels | No payment; strike recorded |
| Tutor no-show | No payment; strike recorded |

### 5.5 Tax Obligations

You are responsible for:
- Registering with SARS for income tax if your total income exceeds the tax threshold;
- Filing provisional tax returns if required;
- Registering for and charging VAT if your taxable turnover exceeds the VAT registration threshold;
- Declaring all Platform earnings in your annual tax return.

SCHOOL will provide you with an annual earnings summary for your tax records. This does not constitute tax advice — consult a registered tax practitioner for advice specific to your circumstances.

## 6. Strikes and Termination

### 6.1 Strike System

SCHOOL operates a strike system for tutor conduct violations:
- **Strike 1:** Warning issued; investigation conducted;
- **Strike 2:** Temporary suspension from new bookings;
- **Strike 3:** Permanent termination of tutor account.

Strikes may be issued for: no-shows, cancellations within the cancellation window, substantiated learner complaints, policy violations, or fraudulent activity.

### 6.2 Immediate Termination

SCHOOL may terminate your tutor account immediately, without the strike process, for:
- Fraud or financial dishonesty;
- Submission of false verification documents;
- Safeguarding concerns involving minor Learners;
- Criminal conviction for offences relevant to working with learners;
- Any violation of applicable law.

### 6.3 Your Right to Terminate

You may close your tutor account at any time through your account settings, subject to honouring all accepted sessions and applicable payout hold periods.
