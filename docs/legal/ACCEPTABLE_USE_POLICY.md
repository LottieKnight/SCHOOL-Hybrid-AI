# SCHOOL Platform — Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 May 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") defines the rules governing how you may use the SCHOOL Platform. By using the Platform you agree to this AUP. Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

You may use the Platform to:
- Study curriculum-aligned learning content for personal academic improvement;
- Practise past papers and review marked attempts;
- Interact with the AI study assistant for educational purposes;
- Book and attend tutoring sessions with registered tutors;
- Participate in community discussion rooms for academic support;
- Manage your personal study planner and academic schedule.

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

### 3.1 Academic Dishonesty

You may not use the Platform to:
- Submit AI-generated answers to formal school assessments as your own work where your school prohibits such use (check your institution's AI policy);
- Share past paper solutions with others in a manner that facilitates cheating in formal assessments;
- Access restricted examination content ahead of formal release;
- Assist others in academic dishonesty.

SCHOOL promotes legitimate study practice. We are not responsible for disciplinary actions imposed by schools or examination bodies arising from a user's misuse of Platform content.

### 3.2 Illegal Activity

You may not use the Platform to:
- Engage in any activity that violates South African law or the law of your jurisdiction;
- Distribute, sell, or otherwise commercialise DBE past papers, CAPS materials, or SCHOOL content without authorisation;
- Circumvent geographic access restrictions or payment controls;
- Engage in money laundering or financial fraud through the wallet system.

### 3.3 Harmful Content

You may not post or transmit content that:
- Is defamatory, harassing, threatening, or discriminatory on grounds of race, gender, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other ground prohibited by the **Promotion of Equality and Prevention of Unfair Discrimination Act 4 of 2000**;
- Is sexually explicit or pornographic;
- Depicts violence or glorifies harm;
- Targets minors in any inappropriate way.

### 3.4 Privacy and Data

You may not:
- Share another person's personal information without their consent;
- Scrape, harvest, or aggregate personal information from the Platform;
- Impersonate another user, SCHOOL staff member, or any third party;
- Attempt to identify, de-anonymise, or contact other users outside the Platform's messaging system for purposes other than legitimate tutoring.

### 3.5 Technical Interference

You may not:
- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Platform or its infrastructure;
- Conduct denial-of-service attacks, penetration testing, or security scanning against the Platform without SCHOOL's written consent;
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform software;
- Introduce malware, viruses, or malicious code;
- Use automated bots, scripts, or scrapers to interact with the Platform.

### 3.6 Tutor Marketplace Abuse

You may not:
- Book tutoring sessions with no intention of attending;
- Submit false disputes or ratings;
- Attempt to route payments for tutoring services outside the Platform to circumvent SCHOOL's commission;
- Use the tutor messaging system for spam or commercial solicitation unrelated to tutoring.

---

## 4. Content Standards

All content you submit must:
- Be truthful and not misleading;
- Comply with applicable copyright law (see the **Copyright and Content Policy**);
- Be appropriate for a platform used by students of all ages from Grade R (approximately 5 years) through Grade 12 (approximately 18 years).

---

## 5. Tutor-Specific Standards

Tutors must additionally:
- Hold genuine qualifications relevant to the subjects they offer;
- Not make false claims about qualifications or experience;
- Treat all learners with respect regardless of their academic level;
- Maintain professional conduct during sessions;
- Comply with all applicable laws regarding the provision of educational services to minors.

---

## 6. Reporting Violations

If you observe content or behaviour that violates this AUP, report it to: support@school.ditshego-ventures.co.za or use the in-platform report function.

---

## 7. Consequences of Violation

Violations of this AUP may result in:
- Content removal;
- Warning issued to your account;
- Temporary suspension;
- Permanent account termination;
- Forfeiture of wallet credits without refund (for fraud or abuse);
- Referral to law enforcement where required by law.

SCHOOL will apply these consequences proportionately and in accordance with the Terms of Service.
