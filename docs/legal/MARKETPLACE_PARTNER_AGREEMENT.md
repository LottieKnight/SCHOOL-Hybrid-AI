## 4. PERSONAL INFORMATION — POPIA SECTION 19 OPERATOR CLAUSE
The Partner acknowledges that, in performing this Agreement, it may process personal information of SCHOOL's users (e.g. name, delivery address, order details, contact details strictly necessary to fulfil an order). The Partner agrees, as required by **POPIA Section 19(1)**:

1. **Confidentiality** — to treat all personal information as confidential and not disclose it except to personnel who need it to perform the order.
2. **Purpose limitation** — to process personal information solely to fulfil the specific order/transaction authorised by SCHOOL, and for no other purpose.
3. **Security safeguards** — to protect personal information with appropriate technical and organisational measures consistent with POPIA Section 19(1)(a)–(c), including access controls, encryption in transit, and secure storage.
4. **Breach notification** — to notify SCHOOL's Information Officer (privacy@ditshego-ventures.co.za) **without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours** of becoming aware of any security compromise affecting personal information.
5. **No further use / retention** — not to keep, use, or process personal information after the order is fulfilled, except as required by law; on termination, to return or securely destroy it at SCHOOL's direction.
6. **Audit & monitoring** — to submit to reasonable audits and provide evidence of compliance on request.
7. **Sub-processors** — not to engage any sub-processor to process personal information without SCHOOL's prior written consent; any permitted sub-processor is bound by equivalent terms.
8. **Cross-border** — where personal information leaves South Africa, the Partner ensures an adequate safeguard (POPIA Section 72) and obtains SCHOOL's consent.

## 5. DATA SHARING MINIMISATION
SCHOOL shares only the minimum personal information required to fulfil each order. The Partner must not harvest, retain, or reuse user data for marketing, profiling, or any secondary purpose.
