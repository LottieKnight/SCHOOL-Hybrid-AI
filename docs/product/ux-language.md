# SCHOOL UX Language

## Tone

Voice should be:

- direct
- calm
- respectful
- academically credible
- operational, not promotional

## Writing Rules

- Lead with the decision or consequence.
- Use short sentences.
- Prefer concrete academic wording over generic productivity wording.
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid childish gamification language.

## Preferred Phrases

- Command Center
- Next best move
- Needs review
- Ready for practice
- Past paper focus
- Proof of progress
- Ask the AI study coach
- Book targeted tutor help
- Revision block
- At risk this week

## Avoid

- Crush your goals
- Supercharge
- Win big
- Genius mode
- Level up
- Personalized magic

## State Language

### Empty States

- "Complete a quiz, review, or past paper and SCHOOL will surface priorities here."
- "Your next review topics appear here after study patterns are detected."
- "Connect deadlines or add planner items to build your academic week."

### Success States

- "Saved to your study record."
- "Review captured."
- "Past paper submitted."
- "Queue updated."

### Risk / Attention States

- "At risk this week"
- "Review overdue"
- "Low-confidence area"
- "Needs intervention"

### Tutor Escalation

- "Get targeted tutor help"
- "Book support for this weak area"
- "Escalate to a tutor if this topic keeps slipping"

## Core Surface Copy Patterns

### Hero

- Eyebrow: short category label
- Title: what matters now
- Body: one-sentence practical consequence
- CTA: specific action

### Cards

- Title should be directive or outcome-based.
- Supporting copy should explain why the card matters now.

## Naming Conventions For Current Product

- Dashboard copy may say "Command Center" while the route stays `/dashboard`.
- Memory/mastery copy should prefer qualitative language in learner-facing surfaces.
- "Past Papers" should remain explicit in navigation and quick actions.
